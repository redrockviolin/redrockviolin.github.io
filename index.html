Meet Jane

Learning the violin should feel both challenging and rewarding.

Every student deserves an approach that's as individual as they are.

My lessons are designed to build strong technique while nurturing confidence, creativity, and a genuine connection to music. Whether a child is picking up the violin for the first time or an adult is returning after years away, each lesson is tailored to the individual.

I'm a Suzuki-trained violinist with a Bachelor of Music from New York University and more than a decade of professional performance experience, including national touring as an acoustic and electric violinist.

Good to know

A few common questions.

What ages do you teach?

I welcome students ages 4 through adult, with a focus on beginner and intermediate players.

Do you use the Suzuki method?

My teaching is Suzuki-trained and classically grounded, while still being personalized to the individual student rather than limited to one method or book.

Do you offer in-home lessons?

Yes. In-home lessons are available within a limited service area, subject to scheduling and a travel fee.

How do I get started?

Email me with the student's age, prior experience, and general availability. An introductory lesson is a great way to see if we're a good fit.
