Good to Know

A few common questions.

What ages do you teach?

I welcome students ages 4 through adult, with a focus on beginner and intermediate players.

Do you use the Suzuki method?

My teaching is Suzuki-trained and classically grounded, while still being personalized to the individual student rather than limited to one method or book.

Do you offer in-home lessons?

Yes. In-home lessons are available within a limited service area, subject to scheduling and a travel fee.

How do I get started?

Email me with the student’s age, prior experience, and general availability. An introductory lesson is a great way to see if we’re a good fit.
